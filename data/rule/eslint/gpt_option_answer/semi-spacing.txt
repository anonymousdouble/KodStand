The ESLint rule "semi-spacing" has one main option, which is an object with two keys: "before" and "after". Here are the details of the configurable options:

1. **Option Name**: anonymous_option_1
   - **Option Description**: Enforces or disallows spacing before and after semicolons.
   - **Data Type**: object
   - **Data Range**: {"before": boolean, "after": boolean}
   - **Default Value**: {"before": false, "after": true}

2. **Option Name**: anonymous_option_1.before
   - **Option Description**: Enforces or disallows spacing before semicolons.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false

3. **Option Name**: anonymous_option_1.after
   - **Option Description**: Enforces or disallows spacing after semicolons.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: true
