The ESLint rule "no-multiple-empty-lines" has three configurable options. Here are the details for each option:

1. **Option Name**: max
   - **Option Description**: Enforces a maximum number of consecutive empty lines.
   - **Data Type**: integer
   - **Data Range**: open-ended
   - **Default Value**: 2

2. **Option Name**: maxEOF
   - **Option Description**: Enforces a maximum number of consecutive empty lines at the end of files.
   - **Data Type**: integer
   - **Data Range**: open-ended
   - **Default Value**: no default

3. **Option Name**: maxBOF
   - **Option Description**: Enforces a maximum number of consecutive empty lines at the beginning of files.
   - **Data Type**: integer
   - **Data Range**: open-ended
   - **Default Value**: no default
