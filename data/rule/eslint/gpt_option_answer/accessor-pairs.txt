The ESLint rule "accessor-pairs" has three configurable options. Here are the details for each option:

1. **Option Name**: setWithoutGet
   - **Option Description**: Warns for setters without getters.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: true

2. **Option Name**: getWithoutSet
   - **Option Description**: Warns for getters without setters.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false

3. **Option Name**: enforceForClassMembers
   - **Option Description**: Additionally applies this rule to class getters/setters.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: true
