The ESLint rule "no-trailing-spaces" has two configurable options. Here are the details:

1. **Option Name**: skipBlankLines
   - **Option Description**: Allows or disallows trailing whitespace on empty lines.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false

2. **Option Name**: ignoreComments
   - **Option Description**: Allows or disallows trailing whitespace in comment blocks.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false
