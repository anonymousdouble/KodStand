The ESLint rule "lines-around-directive" has configurable options. Here are the details of each option:

1. **Option Name**: anonymous_option_1
   - **Option Description**: Enforces or disallows blank newlines around directives.
   - **Data Type**: string
   - **Data Range**: {"always", "never"}
   - **Default Value**: "always"

2. **Option Name**: anonymous_option_2
   - **Option Description**: Specifies whether to enforce or disallow blank newlines before and after directives.
   - **Data Type**: object
   - **Data Range**: {"before": string, "after": string}
   - **Default Value**: no default

3. **Option Name**: anonymous_option_2.before
   - **Option Description**: Specifies whether to enforce or disallow blank newlines before directives.
   - **Data Type**: string
   - **Data Range**: {"always", "never"}
   - **Default Value**: no default

4. **Option Name**: anonymous_option_2.after
   - **Option Description**: Specifies whether to enforce or disallow blank newlines after directives.
   - **Data Type**: string
   - **Data Range**: {"always", "never"}
   - **Default Value**: no default
