The ESLint rule "comma-spacing" has configurable options. Here are the details of each option:

1. **Option Name**: before
   - **Option Description**: Controls whether spaces are allowed before commas.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false

2. **Option Name**: after
   - **Option Description**: Controls whether spaces are required after commas.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: true
