The ESLint rule "no-plusplus" has one configurable option. Here are the details:

1. **Option Name**: allowForLoopAfterthoughts
   - **Option Description**: Allows unary operators `++` and `--` in the afterthought (final expression) of a `for` loop.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: no default
