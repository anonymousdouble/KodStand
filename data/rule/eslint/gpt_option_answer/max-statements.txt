The ESLint rule "max-statements" has two configurable options: "max" and "ignoreTopLevelFunctions". Here are the details for each option:

1. **Option Name**: max
   - **Option Description**: Enforces a maximum number of statements allowed in function blocks.
   - **Data Type**: number
   - **Data Range**: open-ended
   - **Default Value**: 10

2. **Option Name**: ignoreTopLevelFunctions
   - **Option Description**: Ignores top-level functions.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: no default
