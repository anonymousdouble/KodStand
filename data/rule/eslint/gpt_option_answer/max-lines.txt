The ESLint rule "max-lines" has three configurable options. Here are the details for each option:

1. **Option Name**: max
   - **Option Description**: Enforces a maximum number of lines in a file.
   - **Data Type**: number
   - **Data Range**: open-ended
   - **Default Value**: 300

2. **Option Name**: skipBlankLines
   - **Option Description**: Ignores lines made up purely of whitespace.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: no default

3. **Option Name**: skipComments
   - **Option Description**: Ignores lines containing just comments.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: no default
