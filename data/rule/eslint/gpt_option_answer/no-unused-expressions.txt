The ESLint rule "no-unused-expressions" has several configurable options. Here are the details for each option:

1. **Option Name**: allowShortCircuit
   - **Option Description**: Allows the use of short circuit evaluations in expressions.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false

2. **Option Name**: allowTernary
   - **Option Description**: Enables the use of ternary operators in expressions similarly to short circuit evaluations.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false

3. **Option Name**: allowTaggedTemplates
   - **Option Description**: Enables the use of tagged template literals in expressions.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false

4. **Option Name**: enforceForJSX
   - **Option Description**: Flags unused JSX element expressions.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false
