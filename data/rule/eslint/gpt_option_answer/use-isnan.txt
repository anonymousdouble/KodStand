The ESLint rule "use-isnan" has an object option with two configurable sub-options. Here are the details:

1. **Option Name**: enforceForSwitchCase
   - **Option Description**: Disallows `case NaN` and `switch(NaN)` in `switch` statements.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: true

2. **Option Name**: enforceForIndexOf
   - **Option Description**: Disallows the use of `indexOf` and `lastIndexOf` methods with `NaN`.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false
