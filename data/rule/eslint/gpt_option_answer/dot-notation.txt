The ESLint rule "dot-notation" has two configurable options: `allowKeywords` and `allowPattern`. Here are the details for each option:

1. **Option Name**: allowKeywords
   - **Option Description**: Determines whether to allow dot notation for reserved word properties.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: true

2. **Option Name**: allowPattern
   - **Option Description**: Allows bracket notation for property names that match a specified regular expression pattern.
   - **Data Type**: string
   - **Data Range**: open-ended
   - **Default Value**: no default
