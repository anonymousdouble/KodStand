The ESLint rule "no-mixed-requires" has two configurable options: "grouping" and "allowCall". Here are the details for each option:

1. **Option Name**: grouping
   - **Option Description**: Ensures that all require declarations are of the same type.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false

2. **Option Name**: allowCall
   - **Option Description**: Allows calling any function within require declarations.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false
