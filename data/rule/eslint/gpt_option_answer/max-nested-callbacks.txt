The ESLint rule "max-nested-callbacks" has configurable options. Here are the details of the options:

1. **Option Name**: max
   - **Option Description**: Enforces a maximum depth that callbacks can be nested.
   - **Data Type**: number
   - **Data Range**: open-ended
   - **Default Value**: 10

2. **Option Name**: anonymous_option_1
   - **Option Description**: Enforces a maximum depth that callbacks can be nested.
   - **Data Type**: object
   - **Data Range**: {"max": number, "maximum": number (deprecated)}
   - **Default Value**: no default

3. **Option Name**: anonymous_option_1.max
   - **Option Description**: Enforces a maximum depth that callbacks can be nested.
   - **Data Type**: number
   - **Data Range**: open-ended
   - **Default Value**: 10

4. **Option Name**: anonymous_option_1.maximum
   - **Option Description**: Enforces a maximum depth that callbacks can be nested (deprecated).
   - **Data Type**: number
   - **Data Range**: open-ended
   - **Default Value**: no default
