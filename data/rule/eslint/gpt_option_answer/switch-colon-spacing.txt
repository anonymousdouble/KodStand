The ESLint rule "switch-colon-spacing" has two configurable options. Here are the details:

1. **Option Name**: after
   - **Option Description**: Requires or disallows spaces after colons.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: true

2. **Option Name**: before
   - **Option Description**: Requires or disallows spaces before colons.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false
