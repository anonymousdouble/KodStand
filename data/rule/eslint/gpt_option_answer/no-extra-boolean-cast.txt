The ESLint rule "no-extra-boolean-cast" has one configurable option. Here are the details:

1. **Option Name**: enforceForLogicalOperands
   - **Option Description**: When set to true, in addition to checking default contexts, checks whether the extra boolean cast is contained within a logical expression.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false
