The ESLint rule "newline-after-var" has a single string option. Here are the details of the configurable options:

1. **Option Name**: anonymous_option_1
   - **Option Description**: Requires or disallows an empty line after `var`, `let`, or `const` statements.
   - **Data Type**: string
   - **Data Range**: {"always", "never"}
   - **Default Value**: "always"

The rule does not have any nested or additional options beyond this single string option.
