The ESLint rule "no-eval" has one configurable option. Here are the details:

1. **Option Name**: allowIndirect
   - **Option Description**: Allows indirect calls to `eval`. Indirect calls to `eval` are less dangerous than direct calls because they cannot dynamically change the scope and do not negatively impact performance to the same degree.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: false
