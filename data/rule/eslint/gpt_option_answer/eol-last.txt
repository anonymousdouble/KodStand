The ESLint rule "eol-last" has a single string option. Here are the details of the configurable options:

1. **Option Name**: anonymous_option_1
   - **Option Description**: Enforces the presence or absence of a newline at the end of non-empty files.
   - **Data Type**: string
   - **Data Range**: {"always", "never", "unix", "windows"}
   - **Default Value**: "always"

The options "unix" and "windows" are deprecated and should be used in conjunction with the "linebreak-style" rule if a specific linebreak style is needed.
