The ESLint rule "valid-typeof" has one configurable option, which is an object. Here are the details of the option:

1. **Option Name**: requireStringLiterals
   - **Option Description**: Requires `typeof` expressions to only be compared to string literals or other `typeof` expressions, and disallows comparisons to any other value.
   - **Data Type**: boolean
   - **Data Range**: {true, false}
   - **Default Value**: no default

Since there is only one option and it is named, there are no anonymous options or nested sub-options.
